Browser Sonar

Your laptop plays a chirp you can barely hear, listens for the echo, and works out what moved. No install, no server, no data leaves this tab — just the speaker and microphone you already have.

Idle

Needs microphone permission. Use the built-in speaker and mic — Bluetooth headphones cannot carry 20 kHz. Best in Chrome on a laptop.

  1. Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Watch the spectrogram for a diagonal stripe near 20 kHz. That is the chirp.
  3. Hold still ~3 s so it learns the room.
  4. Wave your hand at the screen.
Presence — not running
Radial motion — approach / recede
Gesture — swipe toward or away
Breathing — needs ~20 s of stillness

Range profile

Echo strength against distance, measured from the direct speaker-to-microphone path. Click the plot to pin the bin used for breathing.

Spectrogram

Live microphone spectrum. Each chirp should appear as a short diagonal stripe sweeping up through the dashed band. If this is empty, nothing else can work.

CW Doppler spectrum

Continuous-tone mode. Energy right of the carrier is approaching, left is receding — the Doppler shift read directly rather than inferred.
